# Tava Health

> Tava Health, Inc. is a Utah-founded (2019) digital behavioral-health company that sells mental
> health care as an employer and health-plan benefit. Members are matched to a licensed therapist —
> typically within about twelve hours — and receive care over secure video or in person through a
> nationwide clinical network.

Generated by API Evangelist from the public Tava Health surface on 2026-08-29. This file is
NOT published by Tava Health; it is an independent third-party summary. Tava serves no /llms.txt
of its own (https://www.tavahealth.com/llms.txt returns 404).

## What Tava Health is

Care is delivered by Tava Professionals, a set of state-level professional entities that act as the
HIPAA covered entity. Tava Health, Inc. operates the technology platform as their business
associate. Buyers are employers, health plans, provider groups and benefits consultants; covered
members do not purchase directly. The company states "HIPAA and SOC-2 Type II compliant" in the
footer of every page and publishes a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices effective 2024-08-01.

## API status — read this before looking for a contract

Tava Health publishes NO public machine-readable API contract. There is no OpenAPI, no AsyncAPI,
no GraphQL SDL, no Postman collection, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no gRPC/Protobuf and no
WSDL reachable without credentials.

A developer hub DOES exist — https://docs.tavahealth.com/ — a ReadMe.io site titled "DirectCare by
Tava Health", covering Tava's partner-facing DirectCare product. It is password-protected at the
project level: every path, including /reference, /changelog and /sitemap.xml, returns HTTP 302 to
/password?redirect=<path>. A control probe of a path that does not exist returned the same 302, so
the gate is universal and the existence of any specific page behind it is unproven.

Do not mistake https://github.com/tava-health/docs/blob/main/api-reference/openapi.json for Tava's
API. It is the unmodified Mintlify starter-kit sample — "OpenAPI Plant Store", servers
http://sandbox.mintlify.com — committed with the rest of an untouched template.
